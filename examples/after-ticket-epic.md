# Developer experience

## Purpose

Small fixes to build speed, test reliability, and local setup that no product epic owns. Ad hoc work goes here instead of dying in a backlog.

## In scope

- CI and local build time.
- Flaky tests.
- Local environment setup and scripts.

## Out of scope

- New internal tools that take more than one sprint. Write a true epic for those.
- Product features and product bugs. File them under the product's epic.

## Entry rule

A story needs a problem with a number and an owner before it goes in. Example: "CI takes 18 minutes on every push."

## Review cadence

The platform lead reviews this epic on the first Monday of each month. Stories with no activity for 60 days get closed or moved.
