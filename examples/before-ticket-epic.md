# Epic: Developer Experience (DX) Excellence Initiative

## Executive summary

The Developer Experience Excellence Initiative is a long-running epic dedicated to empowering the engineering organization through faster feedback loops, more reliable tooling, and a frictionless local development workflow. By investing in DX we unlock compounding gains in velocity, quality, and engineer satisfaction across every product team.

## Vision

"Every engineer can go from a fresh laptop to a running app in under an hour, CI never becomes a bottleneck, and a red build always signals a real problem."

## Problem statement

Engineers face recurring friction that slows delivery and erodes morale: CI is slow, tests are flaky, and local setup is complex. Because this work does not belong to any single product team, it is often deprioritized and tracked inconsistently, so we need a home for it.

## Goals and objectives

1. Accelerate build and CI performance
2. Stabilize the test suite by eliminating flaky tests
3. Simplify local environment setup and onboarding
4. Foster a culture of continuous improvement and shared ownership

## Strategic pillars

- Build and CI velocity: caching, parallelization, pipeline optimization.
- Test reliability: flake detection, quarantine process, test ownership.
- Local development: setup scripts, containerized environments, documentation.
